# The judge — how to produce the weekly recap

The judge is not code. It is a prompt plus the profile you wrote. Swap the
model and nothing in the collector changes.

`winnow recap` builds four blocks: the week's findings, the mentality
(`winnow/mentality.md` — the same for everyone), your profile, and this file as
the closing ask. Everything above the line is for you; everything below is
block 4.

## The prompt

<!-- PROMPT -->

> **Write the recap in the language my profile is written in** — that is the
> language I think in, and this is for me to read, not to publish.
>
> Sort what survives into a handful of **categories that come out of this
> week's pile**, not a fixed list. Inside each one, order by what I should look
> at first.
>
> For every entry, the headline is **a plain sentence saying what the thing
> does** — «an encrypted network that runs on 150 bit/s radio», not
> `markqvist/Reticulum`. The name, the stars and the date are the footnote. A
> repo slug cannot be scanned, and scanning is the point.
>
> Then, for each: is it alive (use the verified numbers, never the caption) →
> what it answers of mine → and any doubt worth passing on.
>
> Open with **one** comment on the pile as a whole — what you notice looking at
> all of it, and what I should do about it. One, not one per line.
>
> Close with the discarded, grouped, one line each with the reason. They exist
> so I can see what you binned and correct you.
